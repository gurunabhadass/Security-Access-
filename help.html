Important: The current AccessOne setup is local-device based. Employee data stays in the browser storage of each device. Import/Export can copy employee profiles to another device, but live Login/Logout status and history are not automatically synced between devices.

1. What is AccessOne?

AccessOne is an employee face-recognition and access-recording app. It is used to verify registered employees through face scans and maintain their Login/Logout records.

Face ScanIdentifies a registered employee using the camera.
AccessDisplays successful Login/Logout records and failed access attempts.
VerificationTests an employee face match without performing Login or Logout.
ActivityDisplays a timeline of scan-related app events.
AdminEmployee registration, user management, reports, and settings.
Local Import/ExportTransfers registered employee profiles to another device using a file.

2. Scan Tab – How to perform a face scan

A. Login / Logout Access Scan

  1. Open the Scan tab from the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the “Login Or Logout” scan area.
  3. Allow camera permission when prompted.
  4. Only one employee should stand in front of the camera, keep their face inside the frame, and look directly at the camera.
  5. When the face is detected and matched, AccessOne verifies the employee and creates the applicable Login or Logout record.

B. Verification button

The Verification button on the Scan screen is for a test/verification scan. It checks the employee face match and does not change the Access Login/Logout cycle.

Verification scans can be performed without a daily limit. Successful verification records appear separately in the Verification tab. Failed verification attempts do not appear in the Verification list.

C. Camera result

After a successful match, the employee photo and available details, such as Name, ID, Designation, and Company, may appear in the result panel. An unknown or unmatched face results in a failed scan.

3. Access Tab – Login / Logout Rules

The Access tab has two filters: Successful and Failed. Records are displayed in date-wise groups.

Successful Access ka rule

ScanResult
Employee’s first successful Access scanA Login record is created.
Next scan before 8 hours have passed since LoginLogout is not allowed yet. No successful Logout record is created.
Next eligible scan after 8 hours or more since LoginA Logout record is created.
Third or subsequent attempt after 2 successful Access scans on that day“Already Scanned” is displayed; no new Access record is saved.
  • The 8-hour timer is tracked separately for each employee ID.
  • One employee’s Login/Logout does not affect another employee’s timer.
  • A maximum of 2 successful Access scans are allowed per employee per day: one Login and one Logout.
  • Daily auto-reset and auto-download are not enabled. Previous dates’ history remains available date-wise.

Successful / Failed list

Successful: The employee’s Login and Logout times may appear in one card. If Logout has not yet occurred, a waiting status may be shown.

Failed: If the face does not match, the failed Access attempt may appear here with its date and time. Failed Access records are not included in the successful scan count.

Date-wise Download

Tap the Download button to the right of a date heading. Select Excel or PDF from the menu. Data for the selected date and list (Successful or Failed) will be exported.

4. Activity Tab – Activity and Verification

The two tabs/lists in the Activity section serve different purposes.

A. Activity

A timeline of scan-related events. Depending on the app configuration, it may show successful/failed Access events and Test/Verification scan activity. Each entry may include the date, time, and relative time such as “Just now / min ago / hr ago.”

B. Verification

This is a separate list of successful Verification/Test scans. Each record shows the registered employee’s face/photo and available details (Name, ID, Designation, Company), followed by the Verified Date and Verified Time.

  • A Verification record is not a Login or Logout.
  • Verification data remains separate from Access history.
  • Failed Verification records are not shown in this list.
  • Verification records are grouped by date.
  • Use the Download option beside a date to export that date’s Verification report, where Excel/PDF options are available.

Clear Activity

Using the Clear option on the Activity screen clears the Activity timeline. Review carefully before confirming. This option does not clear Access history or registered employee profiles.

5. Admin Tab – Employee Management

Admin unlock

Tap Admin in the bottom navigation and enter the 4-digit Admin password. The initial password in the current app setup is 1234; changing it in Settings before regular use is recommended.

New Employee Registration (+)

  1. Tap the + button on the Admin screen.
  2. Enter the employee’s Name, unique ID, Designation, and Company.
  3. Select Gender and Date of Birth.
  4. Enter the Mobile Number and Email.
  5. Enter the City and State.
  6. At the Face Scan step, open the camera, keep one face inside the frame, and allow capture/recognition to complete.
  7. Once the face data is saved, the employee will appear in the All Users list.

The ID must be unique. An ID that is already registered cannot be registered again. Face-based matching will not work unless the employee’s face has been captured.

All Users list

  • Use the search box to find a user by Name, ID, or Company.
  • Tap the employee photo to open a full-size photo preview.
  • Use the Edit icon to update employee details/face data.
  • The Delete icon removes the selected employee and their face data. Review the confirmation before deleting.
  • Use the Excel and PDF buttons to export the All Users list.

6. Settings – What Each Option Does

Admin Panel

  • Show profile photo: Controls whether the employee photo is displayed, where supported.
  • Newest record first: Controls the list ordering.

System Related

  • Face match threshold: Face-matching sensitivity. A lower threshold may make matching stricter, while a higher threshold may make matching more lenient. Adjust this value carefully.
  • Face detection duration: Configured time for the face detection/capture process.
  • Login to Logout waiting time: The current rule is 8 hours.
  • Keep activity history: Setting for retaining Activity history.
  • Admin password protection: The Admin area is password-protected.
  • New 4-digit password: Enter a new 4-digit Admin PIN and select Save.

Data & Maintenance

  • All data delete: After confirmation, users, face data, Access records, failed records, and Activity data may be deleted. Export or back up required data first.
  • Reset default settings: Resets app settings and the Admin password to their defaults; review the confirmation dialog carefully.
  • All data export: Downloads an application backup JSON file. Store it in a secure location.
  • Save settings: Saves the current settings and new PIN.
Do not change the face-match threshold without testing. A setting that is too lenient may increase false matches; a setting that is too strict may reject genuine employees.

7. Import / Export All Users – How to Transfer Data to Another Device

Device 1: Export

  1. Unlock Admin and open Settings.
  2. In the USER DATA TRANSFER section, tap Export All Users (.json).
  3. The JSON file will be downloaded to the device. It contains registered employee details, photos, and face-recognition descriptors.
  4. Transfer the file securely to the other device.

Device 2: Import

  1. Open AccessOne on the other device and unlock Admin.
  2. Go to Settings → Import All Users (.json).
  3. Select the AccessOne export JSON file from the device.
  4. Review the import confirmation and confirm.
  5. After importing, check the imported employees under Admin → All Users.
Import does not create duplicate user IDs or overwrite existing profiles; matching IDs are skipped.
Import transfers only employee profile/face data. Live Login/Logout status, Access history, Verification history, and Activity history are not automatically transferred or synced to the other device. Each device keeps its own local records.

9. PWA Installation and App Sharing

Install AccessOne

  1. Open the AccessOne website over HTTPS in a supported browser.
  2. Tap the Download/Install icon in the header.
  3. Select Install in the installation popup.
  4. Confirm if the browser displays its native installation prompt.

Selecting Cancel closes the popup; you can open it again later using the icon. After successful installation, the app may open in standalone mode and the install button may be hidden.

Share

The Share icon in the header may open the device’s native share panel. The shared link depends on the URL configured in the app.

Menu links

The three-dot menu can open the About, Privacy, Terms and Conditions, Help, and Version pages. Each link must point to the corresponding HTML page file with the correct filename.

10. Common Problems and Solutions

Camera does not open

  • Allow Camera permission for AccessOne in your browser settings.
  • Open the app over HTTPS or localhost.
  • Make sure the camera is not being used by another app. Close other camera apps and try again.
  • Reload the page and check the permission again.

Face does not match

  • Use good, even lighting; avoid shadows on the face or strong backlighting.
  • Clean the camera lens and keep the face in the center of the frame.
  • Keep only one person in the frame at a time.
  • Verify that the employee’s face was captured properly during profile registration.

Face engine/model does not load

Initial face-recognition model resources may load from a CDN. Check the internet connection during the first load. Keep the page active after the models load, then try again.

“Already Scanned” is displayed

Check whether the employee already has 2 successful Access scans recorded today. No additional successful Access record is created after the daily limit is reached.

Logout is not available yet

Wait until 8 hours have passed since the employee’s Login. Each employee’s timer is calculated separately using their ID. Make sure the device date and time are correct.

Imported employee is not matched during scanning

Confirm that the correct AccessOne Users JSON file was imported and that the user’s face-recognition descriptor was included. If the employee appears in All Users but the descriptor is invalid or missing, face matching will not work.

Data does not appear the same on another device

Use Export All Users and Import All Users to transfer employee profiles. This is not live multi-device sync. Each device keeps its Access/Activity history locally and separately.

Report does not download

Check browser download permissions and available storage. Required libraries must load for Excel/PDF exports; check the internet connection and reload the page.

11. Privacy, Safety, and Good Practices

  • Use face data and employee details only for authorized workplace purposes.
  • Inform employees about the organization’s privacy policy and data-use process.
  • Keep the Admin PIN confidential.
  • Do not send exported JSON/backup files through public chats, public shared folders, or to unknown devices.
  • Verify records before making official attendance/payroll decisions, and maintain a manual correction process where necessary.
  • Create a backup/export before a device is lost, shared, reset, or its browser data is cleared.
