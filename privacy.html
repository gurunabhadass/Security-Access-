AccessOne

Effective date: 01 November 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how AccessOne handles information when it is used for employee registration and access management. Please use the app only with the authorization of the organization responsible for the device and access process.

2. INFORMATION THE APP HANDLES

Depending on how the organization uses AccessOne, the app may store or process:

  • Employee details entered during registration, such as name, ID, designation, company, gender, date of birth, mobile number, email, city and state.
  • Face images captured during employee registration and access scanning.
  • Access events, including successful IN/OUT records, dates, times and failed scan records.
  • App activity information, such as registration, editing, deletion and settings activity.

3. CAMERA PERMISSION AND FACE SCANNING

AccessOne requires camera permission to capture face images for registration and face-based access verification. Camera access is used when you start a scan or registration capture. Do not use face scanning for a person unless you have the required authorization and notices or consent under your organization’s rules and applicable law.

4. HOW INFORMATION IS STORED

The current app version stores employee profiles, face images and access records in local browser storage on the device. This version does not include an app-managed cloud account or cloud database for syncing these records. Anyone with access to the device or app data may be able to access locally stored information.

5. INTERNET AND THIRD-PARTY LIBRARIES

The app may use internet access to load required third-party libraries from content delivery networks (CDNs), including face-detection and PDF/Excel support libraries. Those providers may receive standard connection information when their resources are requested. AccessOne does not control their independent privacy practices; review the relevant provider policies where applicable.

6. REPORTS AND SHARING

Authorized users may export access records or employee information to PDF or Excel. Exported files are saved or shared through Android or the device’s available apps. Once exported, those files are outside the app’s local storage controls. Handle them securely and share them only with authorized recipients.

7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Information remains in the device’s local app storage until it is removed using available app controls, cleared from the device, or deleted through device or browser data settings. Daily access-record reset behavior may remove daily access records according to the app’s configured workflow. Keep secure backups of any reports you are required to retain.

8. SECURITY

Use the app on a device protected by a screen lock and restrict access to authorized personnel. The app’s local admin password is an access-control feature, but it should not be treated as a substitute for device security or encrypted storage.

9. CHILDREN

AccessOne is intended for organizational employee access management and is not designed for children.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version should be made available to users by the organization distributing the app.
